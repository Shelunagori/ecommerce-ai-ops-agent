# Refund Policy

> Fictional policy for the CommerceOps AI demo (Northstar Commerce). Not a real company policy.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the returned item has been received and
inspected at the Northstar returns centre.

## Refund method

Customers can choose a refund to the original payment method or an instant store credit.
Store credit is issued immediately after inspection and is worth 105% of the item price.

## Shipping costs

Original shipping fees are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or was sent in error.
