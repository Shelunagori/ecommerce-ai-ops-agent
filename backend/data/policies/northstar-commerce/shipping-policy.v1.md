# Shipping Policy

> Fictional policy for the CommerceOps AI demo (Northstar Commerce). Not a real company policy.

## Processing time

Orders placed before 14:00 on a business day are packed the same day. Orders placed later,
or on weekends and public holidays, are packed on the next business day.

## Delivery options

| Option | Delivery time | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3–5 business days | 4.99 USD, free above 50 USD |
| Express | 1–2 business days | 12.99 USD |

## Carriers and tracking

Northstar ships with ParcelPoint and SwiftShip. A tracking number is emailed as soon as the
carrier scans the parcel. Expected delivery dates are estimates, not guarantees.

## Shipping destinations

We ship to addresses in the United States only. PO boxes are accepted for Standard delivery.
