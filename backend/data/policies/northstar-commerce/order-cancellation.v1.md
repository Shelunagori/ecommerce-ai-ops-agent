# Order Cancellation Policy

> Fictional policy for the CommerceOps AI demo (Northstar Commerce). Not a real company policy.

## Cancellation window

An order can be cancelled at any time until it has shipped. Once the carrier has scanned the
parcel, the order can no longer be cancelled and must be returned instead.

## How to cancel

Customers cancel from the order page or by contacting support with the order number.

## Refund after cancellation

A cancelled order is refunded in full, including shipping, to the original payment method.
