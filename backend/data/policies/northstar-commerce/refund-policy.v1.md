# Refund Policy

> Fictional policy for the CommerceOps AI demo (Northstar Commerce). Not a real company policy.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued within 10 business days after the returned item has been received and
inspected at the Northstar returns centre.

## Refund method

Refunds go back to the original payment method only. Store credit refunds are not offered.

## Shipping costs

Original shipping fees are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or was sent in error.
