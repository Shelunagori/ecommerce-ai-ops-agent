# Delayed Shipment Compensation Policy

> Fictional policy for the CommerceOps AI demo (Northstar Commerce). Not a real company policy.

## When a shipment counts as delayed

A shipment is delayed when it has not been delivered by the end of the expected delivery date
shown in the order confirmation. Delays are counted in business days.

## Compensation

- Delayed by 3 or more business days: the shipping fee is refunded in full.
- Delayed by 7 or more business days: the shipping fee is refunded and the customer also
  receives a 10 USD store credit.
- Orders with free shipping receive the 10 USD store credit only after 7 business days.

## Exclusions

No compensation is due when the delay is caused by an incorrect address supplied by the
customer, by a failed delivery attempt where nobody was available, or by severe weather
declared by the carrier.

## How compensation is issued

Compensation is applied automatically once the parcel is marked delivered. Customers do not
need to file a claim. Store credit expires after 12 months.
