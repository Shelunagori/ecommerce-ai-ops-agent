# Refund Policy

> Fictional policy for the CommerceOps AI demo (BluePeak Retail). Not a real company policy.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued within 14 business days after the returned item reaches the BluePeak
warehouse in Rotterdam.

## Refund method

Refunds are paid to the original payment method. Bank transfers may take a further 3 business
days to appear.

## Shipping costs

The original delivery fee is refunded only when the whole order is returned.
