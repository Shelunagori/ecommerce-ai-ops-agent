# Shipping Policy

> Fictional policy for the CommerceOps AI demo (BluePeak Retail). Not a real company policy.

## Processing time

Orders are packed within 2 business days. Made-to-order textiles are packed within 5
business days.

## Delivery options

| Option | Delivery time | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Economy | 5–8 business days | 5.90 EUR |
| Priority | 2–3 business days | 14.90 EUR |

Economy shipping is free for orders above 120 EUR.

## Carriers and tracking

BluePeak ships with EuroFreight. Tracking links are available in the customer account once
the parcel leaves the warehouse.

## Shipping destinations

We deliver to the European Union and Norway. Deliveries to islands may take 2 extra days.
