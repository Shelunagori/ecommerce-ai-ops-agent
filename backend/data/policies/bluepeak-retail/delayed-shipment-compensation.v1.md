# Delayed Shipment Compensation Policy

> Fictional policy for the CommerceOps AI demo (BluePeak Retail). Not a real company policy.

## When a shipment counts as delayed

A shipment counts as delayed when it arrives more than 5 business days after the latest
expected delivery date.

## Compensation

Customers with a delayed shipment receive store credit worth 10% of the order total. Shipping
fees are not refunded.

## How to claim

Customers must request the credit through BluePeak support within 30 days of delivery.
