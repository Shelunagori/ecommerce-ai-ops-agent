# Delayed Shipment Compensation Policy

> Fictional policy for the CommerceOps AI demo (BluePeak Retail). Not a real company policy.

## When a shipment counts as delayed

A shipment counts as delayed when it arrives more than 4 business days after the latest
expected delivery date.

## Compensation

Customers with a delayed shipment receive store credit worth 15% of the order total, capped
at 25 EUR per order. Shipping fees are not refunded.

## How compensation is issued

The credit is added automatically to the customer account when the delayed parcel is
delivered. No claim is required.
