# Order Cancellation Policy

> Fictional policy for the CommerceOps AI demo (BluePeak Retail). Not a real company policy.

## Cancellation window

An order can be cancelled within 2 hours of being placed. After 2 hours the order is sent to
the warehouse and cannot be cancelled; it can be returned after delivery instead.

## How to cancel

Use the cancel button in the customer account. Support cannot cancel orders on your behalf.

## Refund after cancellation

Cancelled orders are refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.
